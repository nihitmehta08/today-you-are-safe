🌙 “Today, You Are Safe” Card

A free, printable bedside card for people living with Alzheimer’s or dementia. Simple words that bring comfort: “You are safe. You are loved. I am here.”

Fill in the names → Download → Print & place by the bedside

This page does not store or send any information. Everything stays on your device. You can re-create the card anytime.

Print on A4 / Letter in portrait and place by the bed.

Created with care in Downtown Cleveland, Ohio by Nihit Navroz Mehta • Free for families & caregivers.

Tip: Read this card out loud in a calm voice, especially in the evening or during anxiety.
