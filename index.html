🌙 “Today, You Are Safe” Card

A free comfort card for Alzheimer’s or dementia care. Simple words that reduce anxiety and bring peace.

Fill in → Print / Save as PDF → Place by bedside

This page never stores any information. Everything stays on your device.

On laptop: choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.
